This is the first post. Everything that follows it is written the same way: one Markdown file, one folder of photos, nothing else.

## What a post is

A post is a text file with a small header — title, a one-line summary, a few tags, the cover photo — and then ordinary Markdown: headings, **bold**, `code`, lists, quotes, tables and photos.

![A photo in the middle of the text](photo-1.svg "Captions come from the quoted title after the file name.")

Photos live next to the post in their own folder. Two or more of them in one paragraph line up as a grid.

> Short, honest, illustrated. That is the whole format.
